Assumptions & Notes
• This calculator uses typical business coaching benchmarks and translates them into a blended “productivity equivalent” improvement on the salary base of your coachees (and, to a smaller extent, their teams).
• Region, industry and company size influence benchmark salary and coaching cost assumptions and a small “spillover” factor for larger organisations. You can override these by entering your own salary or cost figures.
• Use this as a conversation starter and sanity check – not as a replacement for your own detailed data. You can refine assumptions and salary bands as more information becomes available.

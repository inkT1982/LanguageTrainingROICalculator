How to Use This ROI Report

Use this ROI report as a conversation starter between HR, L&D, and business stakeholders. It is designed to help you translate language training initiatives into financial terms that are easy to compare with other investments.

1. Align on Objectives

Clarify where you expect the most impact: improved collaboration, better customer communication, reduced risk, or higher productivity. The scenario settings in this report reflect the focus areas you selected in the calculator.

  • Share the “Your Scenario Settings” section with business owners.
  • Review whether the chosen impact areas match current priorities.
  • Adjust the scenario in the calculator if strategic priorities change.

2. Connect to Internal KPIs

Wherever possible, connect the financial values in this report to metrics you already track internally. For example, you may link the estimated benefit to:

  • Customer satisfaction and NPS scores.
  • Resolution time and first-contact resolution in service teams.
  • Sales cycle length or conversion rates in international deals.
  • Engagement and retention KPIs in HR dashboards.

3. Adjust Assumptions Over Time

The figures in this report are based on benchmark data and scenario assumptions, not on your actual results. As your language program matures, you can:

  • Update participation numbers and cost per learner.
  • Re-run the calculator with more precise internal data.
  • Compare estimated benefits with realised outcomes.

Methodology and Disclaimer

This report provides directional guidance for HR and business leaders to assess the potential value of language training. The results are calculated based on aggregated global data and recognised industry benchmarks. Actual results for individual businesses may differ significantly based on market, customer base, execution quality, and other factors. Speexx cannot guarantee the exact results shown in this calculation.

Key Calculation Drivers

  • Total Cost: Based on your number of learners and the estimated cost per learner (either manually entered or derived from benchmarks that consider intensity, region, industry and company size).
  • Effective Multiplier: Combines factors such as strategic importance, time horizon, industry bonus and scope complexity (languages and impact areas selected) to derive the estimated total benefit.
  • ROI: Calculated as (Total Benefit − Total Cost) ÷ Total Cost.

Use these calculations as input to your planning process, not as a substitute for internal financial modelling or professional advice.

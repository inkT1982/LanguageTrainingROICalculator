Methodology and Disclaimer

This report provides directional guidance for HR and business leaders to assess the potential value of language training. The results are calculated based on aggregation of global data and recognized industry benchmarks. Actual results for individual businesses may differ significantly based on market, customer base, execution quality, and other factors. **Speexx cannot guarantee the exact results shown in this calculation.**

Key Calculation Drivers:

  • **Total Cost:** Based on your number of learners and the estimated cost per learner (either manually entered or derived from intensity, region, industry, and company size benchmarks).
  • **Effective Multiplier:** Calculated using factors like strategic importance, time horizon, industry bonus, and scope complexity (languages and impacts selected) to derive the total benefit.
  • **ROI:** (Total Benefit - Total Cost) / Total Cost.
